Creatine Journey is a personal habit tracking app by SonolDev. We care about your privacy — here's the short version: we collect nothing.

Creatine Journey does not collect any personal data. Your intake records and reminder settings are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere.

Reminders are local notifications generated on your device. No data is sent externally.

We don't share anything with third parties. No analytics, no ads, no external services.

Your data stays on your device, protected by iOS and Android's built-in security.
